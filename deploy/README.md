# Stay Tuned

**The only history class with no spoilers.** Turn the dial to a day in history, step into the rooms of the people living through it, and ask them anything. They only know what was known on that day.

This is the public copy. Saved demo questions answer instantly; new questions are rate-limited, and texting the station runs from our judging-table laptop. Source code: https://github.com/lakshita1212/HackPrinceton-2026
